Hi, I'm Kyra 👋

Electrical and Computer Engineering Student drawn to customer-facing, technical roles spanning cloud, wireless communications, and sensing systems

I am a junior at the University of Washington, Seattle studying Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE). What drives me most are roles that sit at the intersection of technical depth and customer interaction — I love working backwards from what a customer actually needs, then translating that into a real technical solution alongside them. Through hands-on projects and research in assistive and healthcare technologies, I have developed a strong foundation in hardware design, embedded systems, and signal processing.


Lately, I've been building that same customer-facing muscle in the cloud, using AWS to design and ship solutions directly with the people who'll use them, while also gravitating toward wireless communications and sensing systems on the technical side. I'm excited about opportunities that let me stay hands-on technically while acting as a trusted, technical partner to the customer.


In my free time, I love running, baking, and trying new foods in Seattle and back home in San Francisco!
